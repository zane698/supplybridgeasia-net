What we source

9 core sectors + open-mandate custom procurement

Operating as an export trading and procurement coordination desk in Hangzhou, we source across China's major manufacturing provinces according to your exact specification, drawing, or tender BOQ. Specifications, standards, and test certificates are confirmed per order with the producing factory.

Sector 01

Industrial Machinery, Pumps & Fluid

Equipment for water supply, irrigation, mining, manufacturing, and agricultural processing.

  • Pumps & valves: centrifugal, multistage, slurry/gravel, self-priming, and DC/AC solar borehole pumps; gate, butterfly, check & ball valves.
  • Processing & packaging machinery: agricultural/herb drying cabinets, food processing equipment, filling/sealing/packing machines (on request).
  • Workshop & site equipment: air compressors, welding equipment, hoists, jacks, and material handling gear.
Sector 02

Construction, Building Materials & Hardware

Structural and finishing supplies for contractors, fabricators, and hardware wholesalers.

  • Sealants & adhesives: one- and two-component neutral-cure silicone sealants for curtain walls, weatherproofing and insulating glass; PU foam & construction adhesives.
  • Wire, mesh & steel: galvanized and black annealed binding/tie wire, welded wire mesh, fencing, nails, bolts/nuts, and scaffolding couplers.
  • Pipes, conduits & building fittings: PVC/UPVC/HDPE electrical & water pipes, plumbing fittings, doors/windows hardware, and sanitary ware.
Sector 03

Cables, Solar PV, Battery & Lighting

Electrical balance-of-system, off-grid/hybrid solar packages, and commercial lighting.

  • Power & control cables: 0.6/1 kV–33 kV copper/aluminium XLPE cables, solar DC cables, aerial bundled cables (ABC), and 11–33 kV terminations/joints. (15-day quote validity, subject to metal index).
  • Solar PV & energy storage: solar modules, off-grid/hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 battery packs, solar street lights, and PV mounting structures.
  • Power equipment: for distribution transformers, switchgear and diesel generator sets, visit supplybridgeasia.com. Manufacturer authorization letters are available on request for selected distribution transformer (10–35 kV) and diesel generator set ranges.
Sector 04

Paper, Packaging & Bulk Bags

Raw materials for converting mills and industrial packaging for factories and exporters.

  • Jumbo tissue parent rolls (on request): virgin-pulp tissue parent rolls for converting plants; basis weight, ply and reel width confirmed per enquiry with the mill.
  • Commercial paper & film: thermal paper rolls, kraft paper, corrugated cartons, BOPP tape, and LLDPE pallet stretch film.
  • Industrial bulk packaging: woven polypropylene FIBC bulk bags (ton bags), PP woven sacks, inner liners, and pallets.
Sector 05

Industrial Chemicals, Rubber & Plastics

Non-hazardous industrial chemicals, water treatment products, and engineered rubber/plastic parts.

  • Water & industrial chemicals (on request): water treatment coagulants/flocculants, industrial coatings, waterproofing membranes, and silicone raw materials (subject to export compliance & MSDS review).
  • Rubber & plastic products: industrial rubber conveyor belts, hydraulic/industrial hoses, EPDM/silicone gaskets, sealing strips, and extruded plastic profiles.
Sector 06

Mining, Fleet, Auto & Heavy Duty Spares

Consumable wear parts, filtration, rigging, and fleet maintenance items (on request).

  • Crushing & wear parts: high-manganese/high-chrome wear liners, grinding media, conveyor idlers/rollers, and screen meshes sourced to drawing.
  • Fleet & machinery maintenance: heavy-duty air/oil/fuel filters, truck/trailer tyres, brake components, batteries, and hydraulic cylinders.
  • Lifting & rigging: steel wire ropes, webbing slings, shackles, chains, and manual/electric chain blocks.
Sector 07

PPE, Safety, Municipal & Site Supplies

Personal protective equipment, traffic safety, and municipal utility supplies.

  • Personal protective equipment (PPE): safety helmets, high-visibility vests, workwear coveralls, safety boots, cut/chemical-resistant gloves, harnesses, and goggles.
  • Municipal & utility items: ductile iron manhole covers, water meters, HDPE waste bins, road safety cones/barriers, and firefighting hoses/extinguishers.
Sector 08

NGO Relief, School, Institutional & Lab

Supplies for international aid agencies, education tenders, hotels, and laboratories (on request).

  • Humanitarian & relief kits: PE tarpaulins, relief tents, blankets, collapsible water jerrycans, solar lanterns, mosquito nets, and promotional bags/kits.
  • School, office & camp furniture: steel-wood school desks/chairs, bunk beds, lockers, and modular prefab/flat-pack site cabins.
  • Lab & educational equipment: vocational training kits, laboratory glassware/instruments, and general hospital furniture per tender list.

When local contractors, engineering firms, or trading companies participate in government, utility, multilateral development bank (World Bank / ADB), United Nations (UNOPS / UNGM), NGO, or corporate tenders, we act as your China supply partner and sub-supplier — coordinating technical compliance, manufacturer documentation, and multi-factory BOQ pricing from Hangzhou.

01 · Commercial BOQ

Itemised BOQ Pricing in Tender Format

Send your original tender Excel or PDF Bill of Quantities. 02 · Technical Submittal

We assist in preparing itemised Technical Compliance & Deviation Sheets against tender specifications, and compile factory routine test standards, product datasheets, GA drawings, and applicable ISO / IEC / CE compliance certificates per order with the producing factories.

03 · Bid Authorizations

Manufacturer Authorization (MAF) & Factory Documentation Coordination

Beyond our existing manufacturer authorizations for selected 10–35 kV distribution transformers and diesel generator sets (copies on request; for heavy power equipment see supplybridgeasia.com), we coordinate with participating Chinese manufacturers across other tender categories to issue Project-Specific Manufacturer Authorization Forms (MAF) and OEM supply declarations (subject to manufacturer approval; the authorising manufacturer is the supplying manufacturer). Certificates of Origin (CO) are arranged via CCPIT / China Customs where required by the tender dossier.

04 · Post-Award Execution

Multi-Factory Consolidation & Pre-Shipment Verification

Upon contract award, we coordinate production schedules across different regional industrial clusters, collect factory test records and packing photos, arrange optional independent third-party inspection (nominated by buyer or arranged per contract), and deliver unified FCL/LCL shipping documents.

How we work

Clear process from enquiry to shipment

We focus on factual specification matching, transparent written terms, and documented pre-shipment verification.

1. Send your specification, sample photo, or BOQ

Share the item description, technical standard, drawing/photo, quantity, destination port, and required delivery window by email or WhatsApp.

2. Written quotation

We verify availability and specifications with qualified Chinese manufacturers and send you a written quotation from us, based on confirmed manufacturer pricing, stating unit price, validity period, production lead time, and any technical deviations.

3. Order confirmation & contract

Once specifications and pricing are approved, the order is confirmed under a written sales contract and proforma invoice stating technical parameters, inspection scope, delivery schedule, and payment terms.

4. Production follow-up & inspection

We track manufacturing milestones, collect factory routine test records, dimension/marking photos, or coordinate third-party pre-shipment inspection before shipment release.

5. Consolidation & export delivery

We coordinate export customs declaration (Hangzhou Qianjiang Customs code 3301960NBT) and arrange FOB, CFR, or CIF sea/air/rail freight through freight forwarders.

BOM Consolidation & LCL

Multi-item procurement for contractors, bidders & wholesalers

When a project or wholesale order requires equipment and auxiliary materials from several different factories in China, dealing with multiple workshops separately adds communication, document, and freight overhead.

  • Bid & tender package preparation: if you are bidding on a local government, utility, NGO, or corporate tender, send us the equipment BOQ. We collect manufacturer pricing and technical datasheets so you can prepare a competitive bid.
  • Multi-supplier freight coordination: we coordinate production schedules across participating suppliers and arrange LCL consolidation or combined FCL container loading through freight forwarders at major Chinese ports (Shanghai, Ningbo, Qingdao, Tianjin, Shenzhen).
  • Unified documentation: clear packing lists, commercial invoices, certificates of origin, and bill of lading drafts checked before vessel departure.
Pre-shipment verification

Inspection before balance payment

You review the pre-shipment verification evidence before paying the balance (under T/T terms) or before shipment release.

  • Factory test & packing evidence: nameplate photos, quantity count, dimension checks, routine factory test reports, and container stuffing photos shared prior to dispatch.
  • Independent third-party inspection: pre-shipment inspection can be carried out by an independent third-party inspection agency nominated by the buyer or arranged by us; inspection scope and cost are agreed in the contract.
  • Payment terms: typical terms are 30% T/T deposit + 70% against copy of B/L (after inspection evidence review), or irrevocable L/C at sight. Final terms are agreed per order.
FAQ

Common questions from buyers

How do you work with Chinese manufacturers?

SUPPLY BRIDGE ASIA (杭州亚供桥供应链有限公司) is a trading and procurement coordination company based in Hangzhou, China. For selected distribution transformers (10–35 kV) and diesel generator sets, we hold direct manufacturer authorizations (letters available on request). Can I send an enquiry for a product that is not listed on this page?

Yes. The 9 sectors above are examples of common requests, not a restrictive limit. As long as the product is legal and export-compliant from China, you can send us your specification, target quantity, or sample photo, and we will check availability and pricing with suitable Chinese manufacturers.

Why do you operate both supplybridgeasia.com and supplybridgeasia.net?

Both domains belong to the same legal company, SUPPLY BRIDGE ASIA (杭州亚供桥供应链有限公司). supplybridgeasia.com is dedicated to our Power Equipment line (transformers, switchgear, and generator sets), while supplybridgeasia.net serves our multi-category Industrial, Commercial & Project Sourcing requests across all sectors.

Do you publish fixed price lists?

No fixed price list is published on this portal because raw material costs (copper, aluminium, silicone monomer, steel, wood pulp, resins) and custom specifications vary by order. Every enquiry receives a written quotation from us, based on confirmed manufacturer pricing, with a clear validity period (typically 15 days for copper/aluminium cables and transformers).

What payment terms do you accept?

Standard export terms are 30% T/T deposit + 70% T/T balance prior to shipment (after pre-shipment inspection and factory test evidence review), or 100% irrevocable L/C at sight from prime international banks. Final contracting terms are agreed per order.
