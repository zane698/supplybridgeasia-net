What we source

Project materials and industrial supplies

We are not a manufacturer. For each enquiry, we match your technical brief with qualified Chinese manufacturers, confirm specifications and test standards in writing, and provide an itemised quotation with validity and lead time.

Category 01

Industrial Pumps, Valves & Fluid Equipment

Sourcing coordination for municipal water, irrigation, mining, and industrial fluid handling projects.

  • Industrial & process pumps: end-suction centrifugal pumps, horizontal multistage pumps, heavy-duty slurry and gravel pumps, and self-priming pumps.
  • Solar water pumping packages: DC/AC solar submersible borehole pumps with MPPT pump controllers and mounting accessories for rural water and agricultural tenders.
  • Valves & pipeline fittings: gate, butterfly, check and ball valves, flanges, and mechanical pipe couplings.
  • Technical confirmation: pump curves, wet-end materials (cast iron, stainless steel, high-chrome alloy), motor voltage/frequency (50 Hz / 60 Hz), and factory test certificates confirmed per order with the manufacturer.
Category 02

Construction, Chemical & Hardware Materials

Bulk materials and consumables for building contractors, curtain-wall fabricators, and hardware importers.

  • Silicone sealants: one- and two-component neutral-cure silicone sealants for curtain walls, weatherproofing and insulating glass (200 L drums or 300 ml / 590 ml sausages).
  • Construction wire & fasteners: galvanized iron (GI) and black annealed binding/tie wire (single-loop, double-loop, or coil wire in custom wire gauges), wire mesh, and structural fasteners.
  • Conduits & site packaging: rigid PVC/UPVC and corrugated HDPE electrical and drainage conduits, fittings, and woven polypropylene FIBC bulk bags (ton bags).
  • Technical confirmation: TDS/MSDS, batch test reports, wire gauge tolerance, and container loading weights confirmed per order with the manufacturer.
Category 03

Power Cables, Accessories & Solar PV Balance-of-System

Electrical installation materials and renewable energy packages for EPC contractors and local tender bidders.

  • LV & MV power cables: copper and aluminium conductor XLPE insulated power cables (0.6/1 kV up to 33 kV, armoured or unarmoured, per IEC 60502 or project standard). Note: cable quotes are valid for 15 days and subject to copper/aluminium index adjustment until order confirmation.
  • Cable accessories: 11 kV–33 kV heat-shrink and cold-shrink indoor/outdoor cable terminations, straight-through joints, and line hardware.
  • Solar PV & BESS kits: solar PV modules, hybrid/off-grid inverters, LiFePO4 battery packs, and galvanized ground/roof mounting structures.
  • Power equipment: for distribution transformers, switchgear and diesel generator sets, visit our Power Equipment site at supplybridgeasia.com. Manufacturer authorization letters are available on request for selected distribution transformer (10–35 kV) and diesel generator set ranges.
Category 04

Paper Parent Rolls & Custom Project Sourcing

Raw materials for converting mills and custom equipment sourcing for international aid or utility tenders.

  • Jumbo tissue parent rolls (on request): virgin-pulp tissue jumbo rolls for converting plants; basis weight, ply and reel width confirmed per enquiry with the producing mill.
  • Specialty & commercial paper: thermal paper jumbo or slit rolls and packaging board on request.
  • Custom tender equipment (on request): agricultural processing machines (e.g., industrial herb/crop drying cabinets), workshop tools, and NGO relief supplies sourced against exact tender technical specifications.
  • Technical confirmation: basis weight, brightness, ply bonding, and container loading plans confirmed per enquiry with the producing mill or factory.
How we work

Clear process from enquiry to shipment

We focus on factual specification matching, transparent written terms, and documented pre-shipment verification.

1. Send your specification or BOQ

Share the item description, technical standard, quantity, destination port, and required delivery window by email or WhatsApp.

2. Written quotation

We verify availability and specifications with qualified Chinese manufacturers and send you a written quotation from us, based on confirmed manufacturer pricing, stating unit price, validity period, production lead time, and any technical deviations.

3. Order confirmation & contract

Once specifications and pricing are approved, the order is confirmed under a written sales contract and proforma invoice stating technical parameters, inspection scope, delivery schedule, and payment terms.

4. Production follow-up & inspection

We track manufacturing milestones, collect factory routine test records, dimension/marking photos, or coordinate third-party pre-shipment inspection before shipment release.

5. Consolidation & export delivery

We coordinate export customs declaration (Hangzhou Qianjiang Customs code 3301960NBT) and arrange FOB, CFR, or CIF sea freight through freight forwarders.

BOM Consolidation & LCL

Multi-item procurement for contractors and bidders

When a project requires equipment and auxiliary materials from several different factories in China, dealing with multiple workshops separately adds communication, document, and freight overhead.

  • Bid & tender package preparation: if you are bidding on a local government, utility, or international donor tender, send us the equipment BOQ. We collect written manufacturer quotes and technical datasheets so you can prepare your bid.
  • Multi-supplier freight coordination: we coordinate production schedules across participating suppliers and arrange LCL consolidation or combined FCL container loading through freight forwarders at major Chinese ports (Shanghai, Ningbo, Qingdao, Tianjin, Shenzhen).
  • Unified documentation: clear packing lists, commercial invoices, certificates of origin, and bill of lading drafts checked before vessel departure.
Pre-shipment verification

Inspection before balance payment

You review the pre-shipment verification evidence before paying the balance (under T/T terms) or before shipment release.

  • Factory test & packing evidence: nameplate photos, quantity count, dimension checks, routine factory test reports, and container stuffing photos shared prior to dispatch.
  • Independent third-party inspection: pre-shipment inspection can be carried out by an independent third-party inspection agency nominated by the buyer or arranged by us; inspection scope and cost are agreed in the contract.
  • Payment terms: typical terms are 30% T/T deposit + 70% against copy of B/L (after inspection evidence review), or irrevocable L/C at sight. Final terms are agreed per order.
FAQ

Common questions from buyers

Are you a manufacturer or an exclusive agent?

No. SUPPLY BRIDGE ASIA (杭州亚供桥供应链有限公司) is a trading and procurement coordination company established in September 2026 in Hangzhou, China. For selected distribution transformers (10–35 kV) and diesel generator sets, we hold manufacturer authorizations (letters available on request). For industrial pumps, construction materials, cables, and paper consumables, we work with Chinese manufacturers on a per-project sourcing and coordination basis, confirming specifications and certificates per order with the producing factory.

Why do you operate both supplybridgeasia.com and supplybridgeasia.net?

Both domains belong to the same legal company, SUPPLY BRIDGE ASIA (杭州亚供桥供应链有限公司). supplybridgeasia.com is dedicated to our Power Equipment line (transformers, switchgear, and generator sets), while supplybridgeasia.net serves our broader Industrial & Engineering Supplies requests (pumps, construction chemicals & hardware, cables, solar BOS, and commercial consumables).

Do you publish fixed price lists?

No fixed price list is published on this portal because raw material costs (copper, aluminium, silicone monomer, steel, wood pulp) and custom engineering specifications vary by project. Every enquiry receives a written quotation from us, based on confirmed manufacturer pricing, with a clear validity period (typically 15 days for copper/aluminium cables and transformers).

What payment terms do you accept?

Typical terms are 30% T/T deposit + 70% balance against copy of B/L (after pre-shipment inspection evidence review), or irrevocable L/C at sight. Final payment and contracting terms are agreed per order.
