What we do

How we coordinate your order

Different industrial products are made by different specialist factories. We coordinate sourcing, technical verification, inspection, and export delivery:

Written quotations

We obtain written quotes from qualified Chinese manufacturers stating price, validity, lead time, and technical parameters.

Specification check

We verify models, materials, standards, and packaging requirements, bringing any deviation to you in writing before order confirmation.

Pre-shipment inspection

We collect factory routine test records and packing photos, or arrange independent third-party inspection per agreement.

Consolidation & export

We coordinate LCL consolidation or FCL container loading through freight forwarders and handle export customs declaration.
